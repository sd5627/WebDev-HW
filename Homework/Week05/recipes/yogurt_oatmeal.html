Yogurt Oatmeal

优格酸奶麦片 — A refreshing mix of yogurt, oats, and fresh fruits.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 0 mins

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Oats
  • Strawberries
  • Blueberries
  • Banana
  • Honey
  • Nuts

Directions

  1. Add oats to a bowl.
  2. Pour yogurt over the oats.
  3. Add sliced fruits on top.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
  5. Sprinkle nuts and serve.
