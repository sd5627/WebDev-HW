Basque Cheesecake

巴斯克芝士蛋糕 — Creamy, caramelized cheesecake with a rich and soft center.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Cream cheese (125g)
  • Sugar (30g)
  • Egg (1)
  • Cream (65ml)
  • Cornstarch (5g)

Directions

  1. Soften cream cheese at room temperature.
  2. Mix with sugar until smooth.
  3. Add egg and mix well.
  4. Pour in cream and stir.
  5. Add cornstarch and mix evenly.
  6. Pour into mold lined with parchment.
  7. Bake at 230°C for about 15 minutes.
  8. Let cool before serving.
