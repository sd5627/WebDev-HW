Sour Soup Beef

酸汤肥牛 — A spicy, tangy, and comforting dish with tender beef slices and rich broth.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Beef slices (500g)
  • Enoki mushrooms
  • Glass noodles (or vermicelli)
  • Baby bok choy (optional)
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Yellow lantern chili sauce
  • Pickled chili sauce
  • White vinegar
  • Salt & sugar
  • Cooking wine
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Blanch beef slices with cooking wine, remove and set aside.
  2. Soak glass noodles until soft.
  3. Boil enoki mushrooms for 1 minute and remove.
  4. Heat oil, sauté garlic and ginger until fragrant.
  5. Add chili sauces and stir until red oil appears.
  6. Pour in water and bring to a boil.
  7. Season with salt, sugar, and a splash of vinegar.
  8. Add enoki mushrooms and noodles, cook until soft.
  9. Add beef slices back into the soup and cook briefly.
  10. Transfer to a bowl with vegetables.
  11. Top with chopped chili and scallions, pour hot oil over for extra flavor.
