Mango Chicken Quinoa Salad

芒果鸡肉藜麦沙拉 — A fresh and healthy salad with tender chicken breast, quinoa, mango, and mixed greens.

Servings 1–2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 chicken breast
  • 1/2 passion fruit
  • 1/2 mango
  • 43 g mixed salad greens
  • 4 cherry tomatoes
  • 8 g radish slices
  • 25 g tri-color quinoa
  • olive oil dressing (to taste)
  • salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Season the chicken breast with salt, black pepper, and a little butter. Add rosemary if desired.
  2. Cook the chicken at low temperature (around 60°C / 140°F) for about 1 hour, or cook gently until tender. Slice the chicken after it cools slightly.
  3. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add quinoa, and cook for about 15 minutes. Drain the water and let the quinoa cool.
  4. Slice the radish thinly. Cut the mango into thin slices or small cubes. Cut the cherry tomatoes in half.
  5. Place the mixed greens and sliced chicken in a bowl. Add mango, cherry tomatoes, radish slices, and quinoa on top.
  6. Prepare the dressing by mixing olive oil, lemon juice, honey, mustard, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and gently toss before serving.
