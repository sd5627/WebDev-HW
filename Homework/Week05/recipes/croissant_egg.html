Croissant Egg Breakfast

温泉蛋可颂早餐盘 — Buttery croissant with a soft egg, paired with fresh fruits and shrimp.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Croissant
  • Egg
  • Bacon slices
  • Cheese slice
  • Shrimp
  • Avocado
  • Blueberries
  • Strawberries
  • Salt & pepper
  • Butter or oil

Directions

  1. Slice the croissant open slightly.
  2. Add a slice of cheese inside.
  3. Crack an egg into the center.
  4. Wrap bacon around the croissant.
  5. Air fry or bake at 170°C for about 8–10 minutes.
  6. Cook shrimp in a pan until pink.
  7. Slice avocado and prepare fruits.
  8. Plate everything nicely and serve.
