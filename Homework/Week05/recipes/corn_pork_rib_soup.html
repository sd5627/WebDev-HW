Corn Pork Rib Soup

玉米排骨汤 — A light and nourishing soup with sweet corn and tender pork ribs.

Servings 2–3
Prep 15 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs
  • Corn (cut into chunks)
  • Chinese yam (optional)
  • Carrot (optional)
  • Red dates (optional)
  • Ginger slices
  • Scallions
  • Cooking wine
  • Salt
  • White pepper (optional)

Directions

  1. Wash pork ribs and blanch in cold water with ginger and cooking wine.
  2. Bring to boil, remove ribs and rinse clean.
  3. Cut corn, yam, and carrot into chunks.
  4. Add ribs, ginger, and scallions into a pot.
  5. Pour in enough boiling water to cover ingredients.
  6. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  7. Add corn, carrot, and red dates.
  8. Continue simmering for another 20 minutes.
  9. Add yam and cook for 15 minutes.
  10. Season with salt and white pepper.
  11. Garnish with scallions and serve.
