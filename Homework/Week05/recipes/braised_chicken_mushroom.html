Braised Chicken with Mushrooms

小鸡炖蘑菇 — A classic Chinese comfort dish with tender chicken and rich mushroom flavor.

Servings 2–3
Prep 20 mins
Cook 60–90 mins

Ingredients

  • Chicken (about 800g)
  • Dried mushrooms (50g)
  • Ginger slices
  • Garlic
  • Scallions
  • Star anise
  • Sichuan peppercorn
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Cooking wine
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Cut chicken into pieces and rinse.
  2. Blanch in cold water with ginger and cooking wine.
  3. Remove and rinse clean.
  4. Soak dried mushrooms until soft, keep soaking water.
  5. Heat oil, add peppercorn and star anise until fragrant.
  6. Add ginger, garlic, scallions and stir fry.
  7. Add chicken and stir fry until slightly golden.
  8. Add soy sauce and dark soy for color.
  9. Add mushrooms and mix well.
  10. Pour in mushroom water and enough hot water.
  11. Add rock sugar and bring to boil.
  12. Simmer on low heat for 1–1.5 hours.
  13. Season with salt and reduce sauce.
  14. Serve hot with rice.
