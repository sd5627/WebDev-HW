Mushroom Ham Pizza

杂菌火腿披萨 — A crispy pizza topped with mushrooms, ham, and melted cheese.

Servings 1
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Pizza base
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Mushrooms (mixed)
  • Ham slices
  • Tomato sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Italian herbs

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C (428°F).
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly on the pizza base.
  3. Add mushrooms and ham slices.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Drizzle olive oil and season lightly.
  6. Bake for 8–10 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  7. Remove, slice, and serve hot.
