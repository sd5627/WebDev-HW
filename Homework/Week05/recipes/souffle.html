Soufflé

舒芙蕾 — Light, airy, and fluffy dessert with a delicate texture.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs (3)
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Cornstarch
  • Butter
  • Vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Separate egg whites and yolks.
  2. Mix yolks with milk, flour, and sugar.
  3. Beat egg whites until stiff peaks form.
  4. Gently fold whites into the batter.
  5. Pour into pan or molds.
  6. Cook on low heat or bake until puffed.
  7. Serve immediately while warm.
