Pork Ribs Rice Cake

排骨年糕 — Soft rice cakes with tender braised pork ribs in a rich savory sauce.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 40 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs
  • Rice cakes
  • Rock sugar
  • Ginger & scallion
  • Dried chili
  • Star anise
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Bay leaves
  • Soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce

Directions

  1. Cut rice cakes into strips.
  2. Chop ribs and rinse clean.
  3. Blanch with ginger, scallion, and cooking wine.
  4. Rinse with hot water.
  5. Melt rock sugar to caramel.
  6. Add ribs and stir fry.
  7. Add seasoning and spices.
  8. Simmer 30 minutes.
  9. Add rice cakes, cook 10 minutes.
  10. Reduce sauce and serve.
