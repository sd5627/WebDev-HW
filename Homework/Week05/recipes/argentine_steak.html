Argentine Steak

阿根廷香煎牛排 — Juicy pan-seared steak served with fresh garnish and rich flavor.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Steak (ribeye or sirloin)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Optional vegetables

Directions

  1. Let steak rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  2. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat pan until very hot, add oil.
  4. Sear steak for 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Add butter and garlic, baste continuously.
  6. Cook to desired doneness.
  7. Rest steak before slicing.
  8. Serve with vegetables and garnish.
