Herb Chicken Roll

香草鸡肉卷 — A light and flavorful wrap filled with chicken, vegetables, herbs, and yogurt sauce.

Servings 1–2
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 whole wheat tortilla
  • 1/2 chicken breast
  • 16 g yogurt sauce
  • 12 g egg yolk sauce
  • 10 g herbs
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 2 cherry tomatoes
  • 5 g onion
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken breast until fully done, then slice it into thin strips.
  2. In a bowl, mix yogurt sauce, egg yolk sauce, herbs, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Lay the tortilla flat and place lettuce leaves on top.
  4. Add the sliced chicken and prepared sauce mixture.
  5. Top with cherry tomatoes and chopped onion.
  6. Roll the tortilla tightly into a wrap.
  7. Pan-sear the wrap lightly until the surface turns golden.
  8. Cut the wrap in half and serve.
