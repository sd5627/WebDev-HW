Morel Shrimp Steamed Egg

虾滑羊肚菌蒸蛋 — Silky steamed egg topped with shrimp paste and morel mushrooms.

Servings 2
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs (3)
  • Warm water (1.5x egg volume)
  • Morel mushrooms
  • Shrimp paste
  • Salt
  • Scallions
  • Steamed fish soy sauce
  • Cooking oil

Directions

  1. Soak morel mushrooms in warm water until soft.
  2. Beat eggs and mix with salt and warm water.
  3. Strain the mixture for a smooth texture.
  4. Pour into a bowl and steam over low heat for 5 minutes.
  5. Fill morels with shrimp paste.
  6. Place on semi-set egg custard.
  7. Steam for another 3–5 minutes.
  8. Top with scallions.
  9. Drizzle hot oil and steamed fish soy sauce.
  10. Serve immediately.
