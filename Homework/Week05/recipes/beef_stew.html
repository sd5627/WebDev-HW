Tomato Potato Beef Stew

番茄土豆炖牛腩 — A rich and comforting stew with tender beef, tomatoes, and potatoes.

Servings 2–3
Prep 15 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Beef brisket (cut into chunks)
  • Tomatoes (cut into pieces)
  • Potatoes (cubed)
  • Carrot (optional)
  • Onion
  • Ginger slices
  • Star anise
  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Cooking wine
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Blanch beef in cold water with ginger and cooking wine. Rinse clean.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and melt rock sugar until caramelized.
  3. Add beef and stir to coat evenly with caramel.
  4. Add onion and stir until fragrant.
  5. Add tomatoes and cook until softened and juicy.
  6. Season with soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and oyster sauce.
  7. Add boiling water to cover the beef.
  8. Add star anise, bay leaves, and cinnamon stick.
  9. Simmer on low heat for 30 minutes.
  10. Add potatoes and carrot, continue cooking for 20 minutes.
  11. Adjust seasoning and reduce sauce if needed.
  12. Serve hot with rice.
