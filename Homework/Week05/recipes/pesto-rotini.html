Pesto Rotini with Poached Egg

温泉蛋青酱螺纹面 — Rotini pasta tossed with fresh basil pesto and topped with a soft poached egg.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 120 g rotini pasta
  • 1 egg (for poached egg)
  • 2 oil-marinated tomatoes
  • 25 g parmesan cheese
  • 60 g basil leaves
  • 25 g arugula
  • 35 g pine nuts
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 50 g olive oil
  • salt to taste
  • black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Wash the basil leaves and blanch them quickly in hot water, then transfer immediately to ice water to keep the color bright.
  2. Make the pesto: blend basil leaves, arugula, garlic, pine nuts, parmesan cheese, olive oil, salt, and black pepper until smooth.
  3. Cook the rotini pasta in boiling water for about 10 minutes until al dente. Drain and toss with the pesto sauce.
  4. Prepare the poached egg by cooking it gently in hot water at about 63°C (145°F) for around 1 hour.
  5. Place the pesto rotini in a bowl, add the poached egg on top, and garnish with marinated tomatoes, shaved parmesan, pine nuts, and fresh basil.
