Chinese Sausage Claypot Rice

腊味煲仔饭 — Fragrant claypot rice with Chinese sausage, cured pork, and a soft egg.

Servings 1–2
Prep 30 mins
Cook 25 mins

Ingredients

  • Rice (soaked 30 minutes)
  • Chinese sausage (lap cheong)
  • Cured pork (lap yuk)
  • Egg
  • Vegetables (bok choy or greens)
  • Ginger (shredded)
  • Scallions
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Sugar

Directions

  1. Wash rice and soak for 30 minutes.
  2. Add rice and water (ratio ~1:1.5) into a clay pot.
  3. Cook on high heat until boiling, then reduce to low.
  4. When small holes appear on the surface, add sausage, cured pork, and ginger.
  5. Crack an egg in the center.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat for 5–8 minutes.
  7. Mix soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and sugar.
  8. Pour sauce over the rice.
  9. Add cooked vegetables and scallions.
  10. Serve immediately while hot.
