Braised Pork with Quail Eggs

红烧肉鹌鹑蛋 — Rich, glossy braised pork with tender quail eggs in a savory-sweet sauce.

Servings 2–3
Prep 20 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork belly (300–500g)
  • Quail eggs
  • Ginger slices
  • Scallions
  • Garlic
  • Star anise
  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Cooking wine
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Boil quail eggs for 10 minutes, peel and set aside.
  2. Cut pork belly into cubes.
  3. Blanch pork in cold water with ginger and cooking wine.
  4. Remove and rinse clean.
  5. Heat a pan, cook pork belly to render some fat until slightly golden.
  6. Remove excess oil if needed.
  7. Add a small amount of oil and melt rock sugar to caramel color.
  8. Add pork back in and coat evenly with caramel.
  9. Add cooking wine, soy sauce, dark soy, and oyster sauce.
  10. Add ginger, garlic, scallions, and spices.
  11. Pour in hot water to cover ingredients.
  12. Bring to boil, then simmer on low heat for 40 minutes.
  13. Add quail eggs and continue simmering for 20 minutes.
  14. Increase heat to reduce sauce until thick and glossy.
  15. Serve hot with rice.
