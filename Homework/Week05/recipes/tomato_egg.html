Sweet Tomato Scrambled Eggs

甜口番茄炒蛋 — A classic Chinese comfort dish with a sweet and tangy tomato sauce and soft scrambled eggs.

Servings 1–2
Prep 5 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 2 ripe tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp ketchup (optional)
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar (key for sweet flavor)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Green onions (optional)

Directions

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt, and beat well.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, scramble eggs until softly set, then remove.
  3. In the same pan, cook tomatoes until soft and juicy.
  4. Add ketchup and sugar, stir to form a sweet tomato sauce.
  5. Return eggs to the pan and mix gently.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
  7. Garnish with green onions and serve hot.
