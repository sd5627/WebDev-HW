Classic Omelette

欧姆蛋 — A soft and fluffy omelette served with vegetables and cheese.

Servings 1
Prep 5 mins
Cook 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 65 g mixed vegetables
  • 26 ml light cream
  • 8 g butter
  • 12 g mozzarella cheese
  • 2 g salt
  • 1 g black pepper
  • olive oil (for cooking)

Directions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk well. Add light cream, salt, and black pepper, then mix until smooth.
  2. Dice the mixed vegetables and sauté them in a small pan with a little olive oil. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  3. Add butter to a non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour in the egg mixture and cook gently until the bottom starts to set.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the eggs.
  5. Place the cooked vegetables on one side of the omelette and gently fold it over.
  6. Cook for another 30–60 seconds until the cheese melts.
  7. Slide the omelette onto a plate and serve warm.
