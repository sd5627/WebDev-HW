Croissant Egg Breakfast

Buttery croissant with a soft egg, served with shrimp and fresh fruit.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Croissant
  • Egg
  • Bacon
  • Cheese
  • Shrimp
  • Avocado
  • Blueberries
  • Strawberries
  • Salt and pepper
  • Butter or oil

Directions

  1. Slice the croissant open.
  2. Add cheese inside.
  3. Crack an egg in the center.
  4. Wrap with bacon.
  5. Bake or air fry until cooked.
  6. Cook shrimp until pink.
  7. Prepare avocado and fruit.
  8. Plate and serve.
