Pork Ribs Rice Cake

Soft rice cakes with tender braised pork ribs in a rich savory sauce.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 40 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs
  • Rice cakes
  • Rock sugar
  • Ginger and scallion
  • Dried chili
  • Star anise
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Bay leaves
  • Soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce

Directions

  1. Cut rice cakes.
  2. Prepare and blanch ribs.
  3. Rinse clean.
  4. Caramelize sugar.
  5. Add ribs and stir fry.
  6. Add seasoning and spices.
  7. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  8. Add rice cakes.
  9. Cook until soft.
  10. Reduce sauce and serve.
