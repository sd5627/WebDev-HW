Tomato Potato Beef Stew

A rich and comforting stew with tender beef, tomatoes, and potatoes.

Servings 2–3
Prep 15 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Beef brisket (chunks)
  • Tomatoes
  • Potatoes
  • Carrot (optional)
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Star anise
  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Cooking wine
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Blanch beef with ginger and cooking wine, then rinse.
  2. Heat oil and melt sugar until caramelized.
  3. Add beef and coat evenly.
  4. Add onion and cook until fragrant.
  5. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  6. Season with sauces.
  7. Add water to cover.
  8. Add spices.
  9. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  10. Add potatoes and carrot.
  11. Cook for another 20 minutes.
  12. Adjust seasoning and serve.
