British Big Breakfast

A hearty English breakfast with eggs, sausage, bacon, and sides.

Servings 1
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Light cream
  • Bread
  • Sausages
  • Bacon
  • Mushrooms
  • Tomato
  • Avocado
  • Baked beans
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Toast bread in the oven.
  2. Slice avocado.
  3. Boil sausages briefly.
  4. Pan-fry bacon and sausages.
  5. Cook mushrooms and tomato.
  6. Make soft scrambled eggs.
  7. Plate everything and serve.
