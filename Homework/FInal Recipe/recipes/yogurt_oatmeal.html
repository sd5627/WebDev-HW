Yogurt Oatmeal

A refreshing mix of yogurt, oats, and fresh fruit.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 0 mins

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Oats
  • Strawberries
  • Blueberries
  • Banana
  • Honey
  • Nuts

Directions

  1. Add oats to a bowl.
  2. Pour yogurt over oats.
  3. Add fruit on top.
  4. Drizzle honey.
  5. Add nuts and serve.
