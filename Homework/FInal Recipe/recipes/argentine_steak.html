Argentine Steak

Juicy pan-seared steak with rich flavor and simple seasoning.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Steak (ribeye or sirloin)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Optional vegetables

Directions

  1. Let steak rest at room temperature.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat pan and add oil.
  4. Sear both sides.
  5. Add butter and garlic.
  6. Baste while cooking.
  7. Rest before slicing.
  8. Serve with vegetables.
