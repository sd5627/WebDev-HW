Century Egg Congee

A classic rice porridge with pork and century egg.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 40 mins

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Minced pork
  • Century egg
  • Ginger
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Cook rice with water until soft and creamy.
  2. Add minced pork and cook.
  3. Add diced century egg.
  4. Season and serve hot.
