Soufflé

Light and airy dessert with a soft, fluffy texture.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Cornstarch
  • Butter
  • Vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Separate egg whites and yolks.
  2. Mix yolks with milk and dry ingredients.
  3. Beat egg whites until stiff peaks form.
  4. Fold whites gently into batter.
  5. Pour into molds.
  6. Cook or bake until puffed.
  7. Serve immediately.
