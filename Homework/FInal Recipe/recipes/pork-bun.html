Steamed Pork Bun

Fluffy steamed buns filled with savory pork.

Servings 4
Prep 1 hr
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Ground pork
  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Soy sauce
  • Ginger and scallions

Directions

  1. Mix pork with seasoning.
  2. Prepare and proof dough.
  3. Wrap filling in dough.
  4. Steam until cooked.
