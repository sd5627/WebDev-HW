Mango Chicken Quinoa Salad

A fresh and healthy salad with chicken, quinoa, mango, and mixed greens.

Servings 1–2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 20 mins

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • Quinoa
  • Mango
  • Mixed greens
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Radish
  • Passion fruit (optional)
  • Olive oil dressing
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then slice.
  2. Cook quinoa and let it cool.
  3. Prepare fruits and vegetables.
  4. Combine greens, chicken, and toppings.
  5. Mix dressing.
  6. Drizzle and toss before serving.
