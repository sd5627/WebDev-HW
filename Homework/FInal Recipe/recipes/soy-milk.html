Fresh Soy Milk

Traditional homemade soybean milk.

Servings 2
Prep 8 hrs soak
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Soybeans
  • Water
  • Sugar (optional)

Directions

  1. Soak soybeans overnight.
  2. Blend with water.
  3. Strain mixture.
  4. Boil and cook.
  5. Sweeten and serve.
