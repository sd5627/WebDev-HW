Basque Cheesecake

Creamy cheesecake with a caramelized top and soft center.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Cream cheese (125g)
  • Sugar (30g)
  • Egg (1)
  • Cream (65ml)
  • Cornstarch (5g)

Directions

  1. Soften cream cheese.
  2. Mix with sugar until smooth.
  3. Add egg and combine.
  4. Pour in cream and mix.
  5. Add cornstarch.
  6. Pour into lined mold.
  7. Bake at 230°C for 15 minutes.
  8. Let cool before serving.
