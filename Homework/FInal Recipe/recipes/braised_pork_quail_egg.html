Braised Pork with Quail Eggs

Rich braised pork with tender quail eggs in a savory-sweet sauce.

Servings 2–3
Prep 20 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork belly
  • Quail eggs
  • Ginger
  • Scallions
  • Garlic
  • Star anise
  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Cooking wine
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Boil and peel quail eggs.
  2. Cut pork into cubes.
  3. Blanch pork with ginger and cooking wine.
  4. Rinse clean.
  5. Cook pork to render fat.
  6. Remove excess oil if needed.
  7. Melt sugar until caramelized.
  8. Add pork and coat evenly.
  9. Add sauces and aromatics.
  10. Pour in hot water to cover.
  11. Bring to boil, then simmer.
  12. Add quail eggs.
  13. Continue simmering.
  14. Reduce sauce until thick.
  15. Serve hot.
