Yuxiang Eggplant Claypot

Eggplant in a savory garlic sauce with minced pork.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 20 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggplant
  • Ground pork
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Scallions
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sugar
  • Vinegar
  • Cooking wine
  • Water or stock
  • Cornstarch
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Prepare and soak eggplant.
  2. Cook eggplant until soft.
  3. Sauté garlic and ginger.
  4. Add pork and cook.
  5. Add seasonings and liquid.
  6. Add eggplant and mix.
  7. Thicken sauce.
  8. Simmer and serve.
