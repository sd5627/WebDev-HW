Scallion Pancake

Crispy layered flatbread with scallions.

Servings 3
Prep 30 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Hot water
  • Scallions
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Mix flour with hot water to form dough.
  2. Knead and rest.
  3. Divide and roll out.
  4. Add oil, salt, and scallions.
  5. Roll and coil into layers.
  6. Flatten into pancakes.
  7. Pan-fry until golden and crispy.
  8. Cut and serve.
