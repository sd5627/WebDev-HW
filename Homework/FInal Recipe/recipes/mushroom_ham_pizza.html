Mushroom Ham Pizza

A crispy pizza topped with mushrooms, ham, and melted cheese.

Servings 1
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Pizza base
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Mushrooms
  • Ham
  • Tomato sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian herbs

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C.
  2. Spread tomato sauce on the base.
  3. Add mushrooms and ham.
  4. Top with cheese.
  5. Season lightly.
  6. Bake until golden.
  7. Slice and serve.
