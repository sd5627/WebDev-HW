Chinese Sausage Claypot Rice

Fragrant rice cooked with Chinese sausage, cured pork, and a soft egg.

Servings 1–2
Prep 30 mins
Cook 25 mins

Ingredients

  • Rice (soaked)
  • Chinese sausage
  • Cured pork
  • Egg
  • Vegetables
  • Ginger
  • Scallions
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Sugar

Directions

  1. Soak rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Add rice and water to a pot.
  3. Cook until boiling, then lower heat.
  4. Add sausage, pork, and ginger.
  5. Crack an egg in the center.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat.
  7. Mix sauce ingredients.
  8. Pour sauce over rice.
  9. Add vegetables and scallions.
  10. Serve hot.
