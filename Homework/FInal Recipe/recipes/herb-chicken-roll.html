Herb Chicken Roll

A light wrap with chicken, vegetables, herbs, and yogurt sauce.

Servings 1–2
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Tortilla
  • Chicken breast
  • Yogurt sauce
  • Egg yolk sauce
  • Herbs
  • Lettuce
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Cook and slice the chicken.
  2. Mix sauce ingredients.
  3. Lay out tortilla with lettuce.
  4. Add chicken and sauce.
  5. Add vegetables.
  6. Roll tightly.
  7. Pan-sear until golden.
  8. Cut and serve.
