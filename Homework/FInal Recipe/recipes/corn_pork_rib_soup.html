Corn Pork Rib Soup

A light and comforting soup with sweet corn and tender pork ribs.

Servings 2–3
Prep 15 mins
Cook 60 mins

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs
  • Corn
  • Chinese yam (optional)
  • Carrot (optional)
  • Red dates (optional)
  • Ginger
  • Scallions
  • Cooking wine
  • Salt
  • White pepper (optional)

Directions

  1. Blanch pork ribs with ginger and cooking wine.
  2. Rinse clean.
  3. Cut vegetables into chunks.
  4. Add ribs, ginger, and scallions to a pot.
  5. Add boiling water.
  6. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  7. Add corn, carrot, and dates.
  8. Continue simmering.
  9. Add yam and cook.
  10. Season and serve.
