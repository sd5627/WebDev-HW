Eggs Benedict

Classic poached eggs with hollandaise sauce on a toasted muffin.

Servings 1
Prep 15 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • English muffin
  • Smoked salmon
  • Spinach
  • Lemon juice
  • Butter
  • White vinegar
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Toast the muffin.
  2. Poach eggs in simmering water with vinegar.
  3. Sauté spinach.
  4. Make hollandaise sauce.
  5. Assemble and serve.
