Spicy Mussel Seafood Pasta

Spicy seafood pasta with mussels, shrimp, and rich tomato sauce.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Mussels
  • Shrimp
  • Clams
  • Squid
  • Spaghetti
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • White wine
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Season seafood and marinate briefly.
  2. Prepare garlic and ingredients.
  3. Cook pasta until al dente.
  4. Sauté garlic and seafood.
  5. Add tomatoes and sauce.
  6. Combine pasta and simmer.
  7. Season and serve.
