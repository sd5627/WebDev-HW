Classic Omelette

A soft and fluffy omelette with vegetables and cheese.

Servings 1
Prep 5 mins
Cook 5 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Light cream
  • Butter
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Whisk eggs with cream, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sauté vegetables.
  3. Cook eggs gently in butter.
  4. Add cheese.
  5. Add vegetables and fold.
  6. Cook briefly until melted.
  7. Serve warm.
