Morel Shrimp Steamed Egg

Silky steamed egg with shrimp and morel mushrooms.

Servings 2
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Water
  • Morel mushrooms
  • Shrimp paste
  • Salt
  • Scallions
  • Soy sauce
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Soak mushrooms.
  2. Mix eggs with water and salt.
  3. Strain for smooth texture.
  4. Steam until partially set.
  5. Add shrimp-filled mushrooms.
  6. Steam until fully cooked.
  7. Top with scallions and seasoning.
  8. Serve hot.
