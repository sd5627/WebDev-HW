Youtiao

Traditional deep-fried Chinese dough sticks.

Servings 2
Prep 20 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Mix dry ingredients.
  2. Add water and form dough.
  3. Rest the dough.
  4. Roll and cut into strips.
  5. Press two strips together.
  6. Fry until golden.
  7. Drain and serve.
