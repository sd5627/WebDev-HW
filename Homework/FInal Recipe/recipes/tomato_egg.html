Sweet Tomato Scrambled Eggs

A sweet and tangy tomato and egg dish.

Servings 1–2
Prep 5 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Tomatoes
  • Ketchup (optional)
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Green onions (optional)

Directions

  1. Beat eggs with salt.
  2. Scramble eggs and set aside.
  3. Cook tomatoes until soft.
  4. Add ketchup and sugar.
  5. Return eggs and mix.
  6. Adjust seasoning.
  7. Serve hot.
