Pesto Rotini with Poached Egg

Rotini pasta with basil pesto, topped with a soft poached egg.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Rotini pasta
  • Egg
  • Tomatoes
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Basil
  • Arugula
  • Pine nuts
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch basil and cool.
  2. Blend pesto ingredients.
  3. Cook pasta and toss with pesto.
  4. Prepare poached egg.
  5. Assemble and garnish.
