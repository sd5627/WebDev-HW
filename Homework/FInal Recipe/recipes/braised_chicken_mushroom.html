Braised Chicken with Mushrooms

A classic comfort dish with tender chicken and deep mushroom flavor.

Servings 2–3
Prep 20 mins
Cook 60–90 mins

Ingredients

  • Chicken (about 800g)
  • Dried mushrooms
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Scallions
  • Star anise
  • Sichuan peppercorn
  • Soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Cooking wine
  • Rock sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Cut chicken and rinse.
  2. Blanch with ginger and cooking wine.
  3. Rinse clean.
  4. Soak mushrooms and reserve liquid.
  5. Heat oil with spices.
  6. Add aromatics and stir fry.
  7. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  8. Add sauces.
  9. Add mushrooms.
  10. Pour in soaking water and hot water.
  11. Add sugar and bring to boil.
  12. Simmer for 1–1.5 hours.
  13. Season and reduce sauce.
  14. Serve hot.
