Sour Soup Beef

A spicy and tangy dish with tender beef and rich broth.

Servings 2
Prep 15 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Beef slices
  • Enoki mushrooms
  • Glass noodles
  • Bok choy (optional)
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Chili sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Salt and sugar
  • Cooking wine
  • Oil

Directions

  1. Blanch beef and set aside.
  2. Soak noodles.
  3. Cook mushrooms briefly.
  4. Sauté garlic and ginger.
  5. Add chili sauce and cook.
  6. Add water and bring to boil.
  7. Season with vinegar, salt, and sugar.
  8. Add noodles and mushrooms.
  9. Add beef and cook briefly.
  10. Serve with vegetables.
