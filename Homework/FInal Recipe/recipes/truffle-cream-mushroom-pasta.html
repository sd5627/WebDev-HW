Truffle Cream Mushroom Pasta

Creamy pasta with mushrooms, bacon, and truffle flavor.

Servings 1
Prep 10 mins
Cook 15 mins

Ingredients

  • Mushrooms
  • Bacon
  • Penne pasta
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Truffle sauce
  • Cream
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Prepare and slice ingredients.
  2. Cook pasta until al dente.
  3. Sauté mushrooms, bacon, onion, and garlic.
  4. Add cream and truffle sauce.
  5. Add pasta and mix.
  6. Serve with Parmesan.
